Superman

A superhero

Superman is a powerful fictional superhero appearing in comic books published by DC Comics and is considered one of the most recognizable and influential cultural icons in the world. The character was created by writer Jerry Siegel and illustrator Joe Shuster, and first appeared in Action Comics #1 in June 1938.

Superman was born Kal-El on the dying planet Krypton. Just before the planet was destroyed, his parents sent him in a small spaceship to Earth, where he was found and adopted by Jonathan and Martha Kent, a Texas/Kansas farming couple who raised him under the name Clark Kent.

Clark developed many super powers, among them:

  • Superhuman strength
  • Superhuman speed
  • The ability to fly
  • Laser vision
  • X-ray vision
